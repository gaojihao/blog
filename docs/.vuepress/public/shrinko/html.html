Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 20, 2026
Shrinko is built to be private by default. Every image you compress is processed entirely on your Mac using Apple's built-in frameworks. Your images, their contents, file names, and metadata are never uploaded, transmitted, collected, or shared. We do not operate any servers that receive your files. This policy explains the limited information involved when you use the app and why.

1 Your images stay on your Mac

Shrinko compresses images locally using Apple's ImageIO / Core Graphics frameworks that are part of macOS. The supported formats — JPEG, PNG, HEIC/HEIF, TIFF, and BMP — are decoded, re-encoded, and written back on your own device.

We never see your images. Their pixels, file names, folder paths, and embedded metadata (such as EXIF) are not uploaded anywhere, are not sent to us, and are not shared with any third party.

When you quit Shrinko, no image data is retained by us, because none ever left your Mac in the first place.

2 Information we do not collect

Shrinko has no account system and no analytics. Specifically, the app does not:

  • require you to register, sign in, or create an account;
  • collect your name, email address, phone number, or postal address;
  • collect, copy, or transmit the images or files you compress;
  • include advertising, advertising identifiers, or tracking SDKs;
  • record usage analytics, behavioral telemetry, or fingerprinting data;
  • use cookies or web beacons (Shrinko is a native macOS app, not a website);
  • log your IP address (we run no servers that could).

3 File access & the App Sandbox

Shrinko runs inside the macOS App Sandbox. It can only read and write files that you explicitly choose by dragging them in, using File ▸ Open…, using Finder's “Open With”, or dropping them on the app's Dock icon.

  • By default, compressed copies are written to a Shrinko folder inside your ~/Downloads directory. Your originals are left untouched.
  • If you select a custom output folder, macOS grants access through a security-scoped bookmark, which is stored locally on your Mac so the app can keep writing there. This bookmark stays on your device and is never transmitted.
  • If you turn on “Replace original”, the app overwrites the source file in place on your Mac. This is a local file operation only.

4 Settings stored on your device

Your preferences — such as output mode, the selected output folder bookmark, “reveal in Finder when done”, “play a sound when finished”, and your cumulative free-usage count — are stored locally on your Mac (in standard app preferences). This information remains on your device and is used only to make the app work the way you configured it.

5 Purchases & the App Store

The only feature that involves network communication is purchasing or restoring Shrinko Pro. These transactions are handled by Apple's App Store through StoreKit.

  • Apple processes your payment and manages your subscription or purchase. Shrinko does not see or store your payment card details.
  • The app receives only the transaction and entitlement status it needs to unlock Pro features (for example, whether you currently hold an active subscription or a lifetime purchase).
  • Your use of the App Store is governed by Apple's terms and Apple's Privacy Policy.

Because prices are provided by the App Store and localized to your region, no payment or pricing data is collected or stored by us.

6 Crash & diagnostic data

Shrinko does not include any third-party crash-reporting or analytics service. If you have enabled “Share with App Developers” for diagnostics and usage in your macOS System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Analytics & Improvements, Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymized crash logs. This is controlled entirely by you at the operating-system level and never contains your images or file contents.

7 Third parties

Apart from Apple — which provides the App Store, in-app purchases, and the operating-system frameworks Shrinko relies on — the app does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics providers, advertising networks, or external image-processing services. Your content is never shared with anyone.

8 Children's privacy

Shrinko is a general-purpose utility intended for developers and designers. It is not directed at children, and because it collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9 Security

Keeping processing on-device is itself a strong privacy protection: your images are never exposed to network interception or server-side breaches, because they are never sent off your Mac. The small amount of preference data the app stores lives within the macOS App Sandbox. That said, no method of local storage or electronic processing is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10 Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time — for example, if we add a feature that changes how data is handled. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above and post the new version on this page. Material changes may also be noted in the app's update notes. Your continued use of Shrinko after an update means you accept the revised policy.

11 Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how Shrinko handles your data, contact the developer of Shrinko at support@shrinko.app.
